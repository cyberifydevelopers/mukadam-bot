Abhi kya hota hai, aur kya badlega

Email → review → YES wala hissa waisa hi rahega. Sirf YES ke baad ek naya qadam judega.

Abhi

Har DO ki alag file

  • YES pe bank ki tab (jaise MEEZAN) mein ek simple row: Invoice ID, Amount, Sender…
  • Phir ek nayi xlsx/PDF stock report banti hai aur WhatsApp pe chali jati hai.
  • Har report alag file hai. Koi running record nahi, pichla balance yaad nahi rehta.
Naya

Ek chalta hua ledger

  • Har customer + bank ki apni tab, sample report jaisi formatting ke saath.
  • Har naya DO neeche nayi row. Purana record mehfooz rehta hai.
  • Opening balance pichli row se khud aata hai. Value, margin aur DP formulas se khud calculate hote hain.
  • Upar "Current Position" mein hamesha aaj ka stock aur Drawing Power.

Calculation kaise hogi

Har row mein asli Google Sheets formulas honge, sirf numbers nahi. Koi bhi figure badlein to sab khud dobara calculate hoga.

Opening
= us item ka pichla Balance
Polyol: DO-101 ka 20 → DO-108 ka opening 20
Balance on Hand
= Opening + Received − Delivered
20 + 0 − 5 = 15
Total Value
= Balance × Rate
15 × 350,000 = 5,250,000
Less Margin
= Value × Margin% ÷ 100
5,250,000 × 5% = 262,500
DP Value
= Value − Less Margin
5,250,000 − 262,500 = 4,987,500
Drawing Power (upar)
= har item ke latest DP ka jor
4,987,500 + 3,800,000 = 8,787,500
Zaroori baat: ledger mein poori history hoti hai, is liye neeche "Balance" column ka seedha SUM ghalat hoga. Polyol ke 20 aur 15 dono jud jayenge, jab ke asal mein sirf 15 hain. Is liye TOTAL / Drawing Power upar wale box mein har item ki sirf aakhri row se nikalta hai.

Khaas halaat

Yeh soorat-e-haal aayein to bot kya karega.

Naya customer, pehla DO

Bank ki template tab copy hoti hai, customer ka naam aur godown bhara jata hai, phir pehli row.

Ek DO mein kai items

Har item ki alag row, sab ki same Date aur DO #.

DO ka opening pichle balance se match nahi

Row lagti hai lekin ⚠️ ke saath, aur WhatsApp pe batata hai: "Polyol: Sheet 15, DO mein 18".

Rate maloom nahi

Rate cell khali, Value/DP 0. Aap rate likhein to sab update. L/C ke saath invoice ho to rate wahan se aa jata hai.

Same YES do baar / webhook retry

DO ka ID row mein hota hai. Pehle se mojood ho to dobara nahi lagta.

Google Sheets down

Confirmation phir bhi save hota hai. Approver ko error message jata hai, aur entry dashboard se dobara bheji ja sakti hai.

Bank jis ki template tab nahi

Default (Meezan style) template use hogi, aur log mein note.

NO bheja

Ledger mein kuch nahi lagta, jaise abhi bank tab mein bhi nahi lagta.
